## Deliberately not adopted

The direct OpenAI WebSocket experiment was removed. Its two-run result did not justify its higher observed cost; the active path remains OpenRouter as requested. No smaller model, lower reasoning setting, skipped exploration, sampled replacement for required full checks, or reduced validation was introduced to produce a faster number. Agent-authored scripts from an individual benchmark were not installed as universal dataset recipes.

## Still research, not completed implementation

The final source-transfer report proposes finer profiling inside retained pipeline/partition validation and a shared exact indexed-matching primitive with equivalence tests. Those are not implemented or qualified as general speedups. The current guidance and agent-authored indexed verifiers exist; a universal reusable helper does not. Claiming all performance opportunities are solved would be incorrect.

The latest speed report's 100,000/200,000-record before/after comparison isolates source staging. It is not a fresh end-to-end rerun of every scale case on the final harness. Historic skills trials also retain slower Titanic/Penguins observations; there is no universal latency guarantee. No new paid model benchmark, training or inference was launched during this audit.
